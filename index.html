A T-rex with big dreams and a very short reach. Clap, catch, dodge and high-five your way through five-second challenges that keep getting faster.

How to play

Six tiny problems. One tiny-armed hero.

Each challenge lasts a few seconds. Read the command, react fast. Miss four and it's extinction.

  • 👏Clap!Tap as fast as you can
  • ✋High five!Tap when your friend is in reach
  • 🍩Catch!Move under the snack
  • 📸Selfie!Tap when your face is framed
  • ☄️Dodge!Stay out of the shadows
  • 🎂Blow!Hold, then release in the green
